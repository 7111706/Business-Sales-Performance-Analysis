 # FUTURE_DS_01 – Business Sales Performance Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to uncover key business insights related to revenue trends, product performance, and profitability.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Excel (Data Cleaning)

## Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset (Sample - Superstore.csv)

## Key Analysis Performed

* Revenue trend over time
* Top-performing products
* Category and sub-category performance
* Regional sales and profit analysis
* Discount vs Profit impact

## Key Insights

* High discounts significantly reduce profitability
* Technology category drives the highest profit
* Furniture category shows low or negative margins
* Certain sub-categories (Tables, Bookcases) are loss-making
* Sales are concentrated in West and East regions

## Recommendations

* Reduce excessive discounting
* Focus on high-margin categories (Technology)
* Reassess pricing strategy for Furniture
* Optimize regional marketing strategies
* Improve product portfolio by removing underperformers
